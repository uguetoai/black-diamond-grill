Our story

Born in a small kitchen, three generations ago

Black Diamond Grill is a family restaurant. Founder and executive chef Rudy Carrillo runs the kitchen, and his family welcomes you at the door and at your table.

We keep perfecting the recipes we grew up with and create new ones from scratch, for the neighbors who come in every week and the guests who make the drive from Seattle, Issaquah and Puyallup.

“Our family is devoted to serving you the finest foods with superior service.”

Our town

From a coal town to your table

Black Diamond takes its name from coal, the miners' "black diamonds". Many of the families who dug it came from Italy. Dig down through 140 years of Railroad Avenue.

Dig down
Today

Chef Rudy Carrillo cooks Italian-American dinners on the same Railroad Avenue.

1959

The mines have closed, and Black Diamond becomes a city.

1904

The Pacific Coast Company buys the mine and the whole town.

1900

About 3,500 people live here. Italian families from Sicily, Calabria and Basilicata settle just below the train station.

1886

The railroad depot is built. Today it is the Black Diamond Museum, a one-minute walk from our door.

1884

The Black Diamond Coal Company brings miners and their families north from Nortonville, California, and opens the mines.The coal seam

After dinner

Walk one minute to 1886

Head west down Railroad Avenue to the old railroad depot, now the Black Diamond Museum, about 55 meters from our door.

Museum hours: Thursday 9 am – 2 pm, Saturday and Sunday 11 am – 3 pm. Check before you go.
